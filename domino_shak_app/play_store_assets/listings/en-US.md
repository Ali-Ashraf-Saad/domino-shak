## Full description
Play Domino Shak with friends in real time — no account, no sign-up.

• Create a table and share the room code, or join a friend with theirs.
• Doubt a claim or bluff your way through; the game records every call.
• Chat with quick phrases and emoji, and send voice messages up to 30 seconds.
• Adjust sound, haptics, reduced motion, and keep the screen awake while playing.
• Reconnect after a drop with the same seat and hand; host migrates automatically if the host leaves.
• Published community rules, plus in-app report and block controls.
• Peer-to-peer play over the internet; no game server and no account required.

Not directed at children: the app includes chat and voice messages with other players.
